# Harbor Sessions

Studio notes, photographs, and rough mixes from a small analogue room by the water.
Three nights in September 2026, one borrowed tape machine, and a ferry that was late
every single evening.

This shelf is published read-only. Files are streamed from the keeper's own drive.

## Contents

- `notes/` — session log and mix notes
- `photos/` — plates from the room
- `audio/` — a looping harbour sketch (MP3)
- `data/` — the signal chain, as JSON
- `code/` — a small tape-loop sketch for the curious

## House rules

1. Nothing is bounced until the ferry has passed.
2. The room hums at 50 Hz. Use it or filter it, but do not pretend it is not there.
3. Leave the tape machine as you found it: threaded, parked, and dusted.
