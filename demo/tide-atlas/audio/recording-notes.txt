AUDIO · FIELD RECORDINGS
Two recordings, both made at the edge of the tide.

tide-pool-ambience.mp3
  Place: north shingle, beside the anemone pool
  Time:  05:52, one hour before low water
  Gear:  two omni mics, 40 cm apart, windshield
  Notes: water breathing through shingle; a distant oystercatcher at 0:18;
         the recording is unedited except for a gentle fade at both ends.

night-ferry-loop.mp3
  Place: harbour wall, looking across the strait
  Time:  22:10, ferry due at 22:40 (it was late)
  Gear:  single cardioid, low-cut at 40 Hz
  Notes: engine hum, chain against the bollard, one gull. Looped for
         listening rather than documentation.
