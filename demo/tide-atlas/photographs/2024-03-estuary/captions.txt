TIDE ATLAS · PLATES 01–05
Estuary series, March 2024
All frames made at low water, between 05:40 and 07:10.

01-estuary-veins.jpg
  Braided channels over pale sand, seen from the ridge path.
  The green is not deep water — it is the last film of tide
  holding the light.

02-rowboat-in-fog.jpg
  A boat no one has claimed for two seasons. The owner's
  slip is still painted on the stern; the paint has outlived
  the memory of who painted it.

03-tide-pool-barnacles.jpg
  Pool at the north end of the shingle. Counted fourteen
  anemones, open, which the table says should not happen
  in March. The table has been wrong before.

04-marsh-creek-dawn.jpg
  From the same ridge, two hours later, looking inland.
  The creek writes the same sentence every morning and
  erases it every afternoon.

05-field-desk.jpg
  The desk at the field station: notebook, dividers, a scallop,
  a map folded the wrong way. The notebook is not in this
  archive. Some things stay with the keeper.
